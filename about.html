ABOUT RIZO WORLD

The little face
behind it.

Rizo started before there was a game.

Rizo is Taymar Spencer’s independent brand from Pittsburgh. The first face came from death and fire: absence and warmth, something ending and something kept alive. It became a small flame with a stitched mouth.

The original brand story ↗ lives with the clothes, photographs, and people at Rizo Apparel.

Here, you raise one.

Rizo World begins with an egg in a rainy forest. You take it home. You feed it, clean it, rest with it, train it, and get to know the particular creature that comes out.

The Den is the center. The House makes room for other Rizos. The Arcade gives you small things to do together. Defense brings your Rizo into a strategy game. Dungeon takes that same Rizo somewhere unfamiliar and brings it back.

Two addresses. One Rizo.

rizo.store is Rizo in the physical world: apparel, photography, people, places, and commerce.

rizo.world is somewhere to enter: care, play, progress, and stories. There is no shared checkout or linked account, and clothing purchases do not unlock game advantages.

A world being authored.

The playable Dungeon is currently The Threshold, a short proof episode. Its larger story is being developed slowly. Proposed future chapters are not promises of features already in the game.
