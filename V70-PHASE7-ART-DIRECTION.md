# Rizo Defense v70 — Battlefield Art Cohesion

## Hierarchy
1. Threats and active combat
2. Rizo towers and legal interaction space
3. Route
4. World identity
5. Decorative atmosphere

The route is intentionally quieter than units. World decoration communicates biome and strategy without looking tappable unless it actually is.

## Grounding
Rizos are treated as characters standing on terrain, not stickers centered in a DOM box. Their visual anchor is the feet. Ground shadows and bases stay low-contrast so they provide physicality without becoming a second card frame.

## Range
Range is a soft terrain surface: translucent fill, thin edge, and world-adaptive contrast. It is intentionally not a bright dashed debug circle.

## Placement
Persistent build pockets look like part of each world. The transient placement preview supplies the stronger interaction language: footprint, range, valid/invalid state, and release label.

## World signatures
- Grove — living roots/vines and organic ground pockets.
- Ember — vents, scorched crack, lava crater, warmer route material.
- Moon — orbital cue, craters, basin, cool silver-purple contrast.
- Storm — charged puddles, wind direction, pylons, cyan-dark gameplay edge.
- Blizzard — snowbanks, ice crack/shelf, dark outlines that survive bright snow.
- Eclipse — shadow lane, reveal zones, obelisks, pale gameplay outlines that survive the dark field.

## Affordance rule
Decorative world objects remain quiet. Interaction is reserved for consistent motion, state accent, local placement surfaces, selected range, and HUD/control components.
