What was actually fixed in this inspection hotfix
- Balloons now have stronger body shape, a clearer neck/knot/string read, extra seam/cheek detail, and light enemy-type personality in the face.
- Burned balloons now carry better scorch, ember and soot treatment.
- Ember projectiles now read as a real burning tack/flame shard instead of a generic orange shot.
- Ember impacts were upgraded with a scorched ring, flame tongues and cinder/smoke notes.
- DOM fallback got matching Ember projectile / impact polish.

Harsh notes still holding the game below an all-8 bar
- Balloons are now passable but still not iconic enough. Some enemy classes still lean “cute placeholder with polish” rather than “signature Rizo Defense enemy.”
- Particles are acceptable, but still the least authored layer. They clarify combat, but they do not yet create memorable spectacle.
- Rizo combat motion is improved, but still more snap/recoil than full performance animation.
- Pine Bend works, but it still has room for stronger authored focal moments and route storytelling.

Recommended Phase 2 continuation point
- Enemy family pass: make base/fleet/lead/brick/shell/split/storm feel like a designed lineup, not one base balloon with variants.
- Ember prestige pass: push lingering cinders, hotter impact beats, and stronger burn-state ownership.
- Particle prestige pass: make pops, DOT states and kill moments feel like deliberate authored punctuation.
