VERIFIED DEFECTS FOUND AND FIXED
1. Duplicate restored enemy IDs.
   Risk: enemyMap target aliasing could make restored projectile/status references ambiguous.
   Fix: normalizeDefenseCheckpoint canonicalizes restored enemy IDs uniquely.

2. Contradictory packet cursor vs spawnQueue.
   Risk: a correctly signed malformed checkpoint could restore packetIndex past all packets while spawnQueue still contained threats, permanently stalling the scheduler.
   Fix: normalizer derives packet remainder, compares it with spawnQueue, preserves the more conservative threat set, rebuilds a coherent packet when necessary, and records a validation warning.

3. Impossible waveResolved / waveTotal accounting.
   Risk: signed malformed continuation could restore counts such as resolved=100000,total=0 and create impossible completion state.
   Fix: normalizer constrains resolution accounting against canonical outstanding threats and the checkpoint wave-entity budget.

4. Missing unresolved threats could auto-credit a wave.
   Risk: queue/enemy arrays could be empty while waveResolved < waveTotal; prior isWaveFullyResolved only inspected scheduler/live arrays, so the wave could clear anyway.
   Fix: completion now additionally requires waveResolved >= waveTotal.

5. Reached/cleared wave gap could jump permanent credit.
   Risk: a signed currentWave far ahead of clearedWave could eventually set clearedWave directly to that far-ahead value.
   Fix: trusted checkpoint reached progress is constrained to clearedWave + 1.

6. Unfinished checkpoint mislabeled planning/wave-complete could skip the unfinished wave.
   Risk: START could overwrite the unfinished queue and advance currentWave again; eventual completion could jump cleared progress.
   Fix: unfinished tactical continuation is forced to PAUSED even if its saved phase is noncombat.

7. Already-cleared progress carrying stale combat payload could deadlock.
   Risk: currentWave <= clearedWave prevents completeDefenseWave, leaving restored combat with no legal clear transition.
   Fix: stale combat payload for already-cleared progress is stripped and restored to a noncombat cleared/planning state.

8. Wave 250 could be replay-started after wave 250 was already cleared.
   Risk: currentWave remained 250 due the hard clamp, but combat could start again and could never receive normal sequential clear credit.
   Fix: startDefenseWave rejects starts once clearedWave >= MAX_SUPPORTED_WAVE.
