# Rizo Dungeon — Story Spine v0.4

**Status:** owner canon (October 2026 brief). Where this disagrees with v0.1–v0.3 it governs; everything it does not touch keeps its earlier status. Implementation contract: [Rizo-Dungeon-Contracts-v0.4.md](Rizo-Dungeon-Contracts-v0.4.md).

## Premise (one sentence)

While YOU ran into the store, three bumbling hoods stole Rizo from your car for **The Boss** — a faceless man in a suit who is collecting every Rizo there is — and when Rizo leaps from their van and falls through a storm drain into the Below, he has to climb home to YOU with the help of cave folk who have lost their own lights to the same man, while The Boss's **collectors** hunt for him in the dark.

## What changed from v0.3 (and why)

| v0.3 rule | v0.4 canon |
|---|---|
| N-03 "why anyone wants Rizos stays OPEN" | The Boss **collects every Rizo**. *Why* he collects them stays secret until the finale. |
| N-05 no name/company/logo/face | Still no name and no face. He has a **company mark** (a bell jar with a light shut inside) on his calling cards, his collectors and his caller ID. |
| N-06 the Boss does not know about the Below | His **collectors raid the Below** for anything that glows. The cave folk have lost lamps, fire and people to them. |
| §3.6 caller symbol is unexplained, never reused | The caller symbol **is** his mark. It turns up wherever a Rizo goes missing. |

Unchanged and still binding: every accepted opening line (v0.3 §4.1), "Be good.", the dome light, `home?`, profanity ceiling, Rizo understands tone not words, YOU never learns of the theft on screen.

## The Boss — reveal ladder (never skip a rung)

1. **Voice only:** the caller on the hoods' phone (van, then the dropped phone at the roadside). Speaker id `boss`; his portrait is only his mark on a dark phone screen.
2. **Hands, cufflinks, tie:** comic close-up `boss-hands`, after Rizo leaves the Slip. He learns Rizo fell, and sends the collectors down.
3. **Silhouette behind glass:** comic `boss-glass`, at the Window Hall boundary. A shape behind frosted glass, his mark on the door.
4. **Face:** never before the finale.

Restraint rule: below ground he is felt, not explained — collector patrols, cold lamps, his calling cards where things were taken, a crackle of his voice on a collector's radio, and the cave folk going quiet when someone says "collectors".

**Visual language:** The Boss = cold white light and black. Rizo = warm orange. Everything the collectors touch goes cold: latches freeze, hearths go out. Rizo's warmth undoes it. That is why the cave folk need him, and why the Boss wants him.

## The hoods

Still the funniest people in the game. Incompetent, and terrified of The Boss; the fear *is* the joke ("Done like finished, or done like DONE?"). Their accepted lines stay word for word. The capped hood stays almost silent and is the only one who knows procedure (he leaves the calling card on the seat).

## Arc ahead (set up, not built)

Chase (the window opens on collectors) → caught and caged → The Boss's building → a room full of other Rizos → escape alone, a promise to come back → vents: each grate a darker room (The Boss, his scientists, the scale of the collection) → stealth descent into the factory. The late-game kit (`modes/dungeon/kit/`) proves these systems in a dev-only greybox.
