Local progress and virtual items

Pet progress saves on your device, with no automatic account recovery. Keep an exported backup before clearing website data or moving devices. The game’s Embers, shards, cosmetics, ranks, and other virtual items have no cash value or real-world redemption.

Rizo Apparel purchases take place on a separate website under that store’s terms. A clothing purchase grants no game advantage in this release.

Fair play

Do not attack the service, impersonate the brand, redistribute the game as your own, or use it for unlawful activity. Do not automate advertising interactions or create artificial advertising traffic.

Updates and availability

The game is in active development. Features may change and interruptions can happen. Save backups and the recovery tools are there to help preserve your timeline; a browser clearing its own data can still remove local progress.

Ads and external links

No third-party ads are enabled in this release. Any future rewarded advertising must be optional. Links to the apparel store and other websites lead to separate services with their own terms.
