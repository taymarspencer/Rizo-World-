# V75 — Arcade Revival Product Pass

## Why this exists
The previous Arcade had two clear pillars—Rizo Defense and Ember Beat—plus several charming activities that still behaved like prototypes. v75 keeps their instantly understandable controls but gives the lightweight games an actual mastery loop, then adds two missing play styles.

The target feeling is deliberately old/new: the readability and immediacy of small games people remember from handhelds, arcades, Flash, early phones, and browser games, plus features childhood versions often lacked: forgiving lives, permanent progression, richer score breakdowns, changing rules, rare events, instant retries, and a pet that actually grows because you played.

## Product rules introduced
1. **Fun first; training underneath.** Skill gains must never be the only reason to play.
2. **One-thumb readability.** The control idea should be understood in seconds.
3. **Mastery over clutter.** Add depth through timing, streaks, rules, physics, and decisions rather than more buttons.
4. **Recoverable mistakes.** Where appropriate, runs get hearts/lives so one bad touch does not erase momentum.
5. **A run should tell a story.** Perfect streaks, decoys read, lunch orders, reverse rounds, threaded gates, and wall levels create memorable moments beyond a raw number.
6. **The Arcade belongs to Rizo.** Permanent skills, Heat, Embers, pet reactions, special-Rizo signals, personal bests, and instant replay connect games to the larger world.
7. **Cheap spectacle.** Prefer a few expressive objects and strong feedback over entity spam.

## Preserved intentionally
- Ember Beat: already has enough mechanical identity to remain a pillar.
- Rain Walk: already contains routes, weather, finds, and exploration identity.
- Rizo Defense v74: no gameplay rewrite in this pass while the separate BTD5 pacing study is pending.

## Next product opportunities
Do not add more cabinets simply to raise the count. Future additions should occupy a missing fantasy/control space. Strong candidates are a tiny stunt/racing game, a fishing/treasure game with tactile tension, or a cooperative/async Garden challenge—but only after real-device playtesting shows which current cabinet players voluntarily replay.
