A place around the game

The Threshold has a hand

A limited palette, flat shading, stable drawn wobble, hard little pools of light. Tape on a lantern. An odd sock. Places that have been used and repaired.

The pet stays at the center

Training and game modes now have their own boundaries. They borrow a snapshot of your Rizo and return earned growth through the hub; they don’t write a second pet or wallet.

The save system keeps backups and old migration sources, protects against another tab overwriting your progress, and gives interrupted Defense and Dungeon runs a safe continuation.
